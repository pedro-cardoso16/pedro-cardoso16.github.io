Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Invoice Extractor ("we", "our", or "the application"), developed by Pedro Henrique Pereira Cardoso, collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our service.

1. Information We Access

AI Invoice Extractor connects to your Google Workspace account via OAuth 2.0 to perform autonomous invoice processing. We only access the following services upon your explicit permission:

  • Google Drive: We access files in user-designated folders strictly to read invoice documents (PDFs, images) for data extraction and archive processed files.
  • Google Sheets: We access spreadsheets specified by you strictly to append structured invoice data (such as vendor name, totals, and invoice numbers).

2. How We Use Information

The information retrieved from your Google Drive is used solely for extracting invoice metadata and updating your Google Sheets. We do not use your documents or spreadsheet data for advertising, profiling, or public distribution.

3. Data Storage and Security

AI Invoice Extractor processes your documents in self-hosted or user-managed execution environments. Your authentication tokens and configuration settings are stored locally within your secure environment and are not collected, retained, or hosted on external developer servers.

4. Third-Party Cloud AI Services & Important Disclaimer

To extract metadata from invoice documents, the application transmits document text, coordinates, and embedded images to third-party cloud-based Large Language Model (LLM) and Machine Learning APIs (such as OpenAI, Google Cloud Gemini, or user-configured endpoints).

THIRD-PARTY PRIVACY CAVEAT & DISCLAIMER: While we select reputable API providers and transmit data securely via encrypted HTTPS connections, once data is transmitted to an external cloud AI provider, its processing, storage, and retention are subject entirely to that third party's independent privacy policies, terms of service, and technical safeguards. We do not own, operate, or control these third-party cloud services, and we cannot guarantee, supervise, or assume liability for their data handling, confidentiality, or privacy practices. Users (including those utilizing "Bring Your Own Credentials" or custom API keys) are strongly advised to review the privacy policies and enterprise terms of their configured cloud AI providers before processing sensitive documents.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

AI Invoice Extractor's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking Access

You can revoke the application's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.
